1. First 5 minutes

  1. Open the app (Windows: shop_app.exe; Android: the APK). It works with no internet.
  2. Sign in with your phone number and password. After the first sign-in you can set a 4–6 digit PIN to unlock faster.
  3. You land on the screen for your job — a counter opens on Quick Sale, everyone else on Today.
  4. The pill at the top right shows sync state: Synced, Syncing 3 changes, or Offline, 12 waiting. You can keep working either way.
  5. Press Ctrl K (Windows) to search any job, invoice, phone or customer.
Everything you type is saved to this device instantly and uploads when the network returns. You do not wait for the internet to do a sale.

4. Today (home)

Four numbers that are all doors — tap one to see the list behind it:

  • In production — jobs currently being made.
  • Overdue — jobs past their due date.
  • Due today — jobs promised today.
  • Waiting for you — tasks in your pool.

Below them: the cash drawer (open it, see cash taken, close it with a count) and your overdue / due-today work. When there is nothing, it says what will appear and when — not "no data".

5. Quick sale (counter)

  1. Tap a quick-service chip — Copy, Print, Scan, Laminate, Binding — or type a description.
  2. Set quantity and unit price. Each line's total shows live.
  3. Optionally add a customer name (walk-in works without one).
  4. Choose payment: Cash (enter tendered → change is calculated), Card, or Transfer.
  5. Confirm. The sale gets a number (QS-SHJ1-PC1-00001) and uploads when online.
A quick sale is a fact once rung — it cannot be edited. A correction is a refund (being added). Cash is recorded on the sale but does not yet open the cash drawer automatically.

6. Orders & quotes

  1. Ctrl N or New order. Type the customer's phone — it fills the customer or starts a new one.
  2. Add an item: pick the product, then the options (size, paper, GSM, sides, colour, finishing). The price bar updates live with every tap.
  3. Set quantity, artwork (attach a file or "needs design"), priority and due date.
  4. Either Save as quote (valid 7 days) or Confirm order (locks the price, prints a job ticket, offers a WhatsApp message).

An order has one job number and one customer, with one or more items. Its status is derived from its items, so the job is Ready only when every item is. Tap any order for its full timeline — who did what, when, notes, proofs and messages sent.

Prices are never typed by hand — they come from the price book. A discount above your limit needs a manager. Every price change is audited.

7. Customers

  • Customers are found by phone number — partial is fine.
  • Tap a customer for the statement: what they owe, every invoice with its balance, the jobs behind them, notes.
  • Credit limit and outstanding balance are shown; over-limit is flagged with a warning icon + "Over limit" (never colour alone).
  • Take deposit records money paid before an invoice exists.
  • WhatsApp opens a pre-filled message; Statement PDF writes a PDF; Export writes the customer list to Excel — all offline.

9. Proofs & customer approval

  1. The designer attaches the proof file and sends it.
  2. The app uploads a compressed copy to the shop's own storage and opens WhatsApp with an approval link.
  3. The customer taps Approve or Request changes on a simple page (no login — the link is the key).
  4. The decision is recorded permanently — who, when, what version, and any change request. This is the shop's evidence in a later dispute.
Approving a proof unlocks the production tasks. A rejected proof needs a note of what to change. A decided proof cannot be re-decided — send a new version.

10. Prices & catalogue

  • Prices — the price book. Edit product prices, add quantity breaks (e.g. 500 cards = AED 122), bulk-adjust ("raise all stickers 8%"). Every edit is audited.
  • Production setup — the paper (materials), machines (click rates) and finishing operations the pricing engine uses. Edit them here — no developer needed.
  • Customer tiers — Trade customers pay less than retail for the same job. Per-tier rates are one map, extended from Settings later.

Pricing works three ways, all owner-editable: cost-plus (material + print + finishing × markup), price table (your price list), and vendor markup (their cost + your margin). The breakdown on every quote shows the whole sum, line by line.

11. Invoices, payments, till, deposits

  • Invoice — issued from a confirmed order. A tax invoice is immutable: to fix it, issue a credit note, then a refund.
  • Payment — cash, card, bank transfer, cheque. Payments are the source of truth for a customer's balance.
  • Till — open with a float, close with a counted amount; the expected cash (float + cash in − cash out) and the variance are calculated on the server.
  • Deposit — money taken before an invoice; shown on the customer's balance and applied to invoices later.
  • Credit limit — an invoice that would push a customer over their limit is blocked (owner/manager can override).
  • Expense — petty cash (courier, taxi, materials…), cash comes out of the open drawer.

12. Vendors (outsourced work)

An order item can be outsourced: choose a vendor, set their cost, and the customer price is their cost + your markup.

Vendor jobs appear on the production board → Vendors, so outsourced work is visible beside in-house work.

13. Reports & exports

  • VAT report — sales, output VAT, credited VAT, net due, collected, refunded — with a CSV for the accountant.
  • Receivables ageing — 0–30 / 31–60 / 60+ days.
  • Till history — every closed shift with float, expected, counted and variance.
  • Exports — customers to Excel (offline); quotes, invoices, job tickets (with QR) and statements to PDF.
Reports read whole-shop figures, so they need the network. Everything else — lists, search, pricing, documents — works offline.

14. Settings (owner)

  • Company profile — registered name, TRN, address, phone, email (printed on documents).
  • VAT rate and number prefixes (JOB / INV / QT). Issued invoices keep the rate they were made with.
  • Staff — add people, set roles, reset passwords and PINs, disable when someone leaves.

15. Offline & sync

  • The app reads and writes only the local, encrypted database — it never waits on the network.
  • Every action writes the change and an outbox record in one step; when online, changes upload in order and the server re-checks every permission.
  • The sync pill shows where you stand. Tap it for last-sync time and a Sync now button.
  • An offline session is valid up to 7 days. Two counters claiming the same task offline: the first to reach the server wins; the other is told.
  • A change the server cannot merge goes to Needs attention (the conflicts inbox), where a manager sees both versions side by side.

16. Backups

Run python tools/backup_d1.py from the repo to export every database to backups/ with a timestamp. Copy the folder to Google Drive or the shop's backup drive and keep 90 days. D1's Time Travel is a restore window, not a backup — the export is the backup.

17. Troubleshooting & FAQ

The header text is wrapping one letter per line

Fixed in the current build — update the app. (A button was forcing itself full-width and squeezing the title.)

"Over limit" on a customer

Their outstanding balance is past their credit limit. An invoice that would exceed it is blocked; the owner or a manager can override. Take a deposit or payment to bring them under.

A sale/order/invoice number has "T-" in front

That device was offline and used a temporary number. It swaps to the real number automatically when it syncs.

Can't edit an issued invoice

By design. Correct it with a credit note then a refund — the original never changes, so the audit trail is honest.

The proof link doesn't open

Proof files are kept for 60 days. Older proofs show the thumbnail and the approval record, but the file is purged to keep the database small. Keep original artwork in the shop's own folder.

Reports are empty / need internet

Reports aggregate the whole shop, so they read from the server and need a connection. Lists and pricing work offline.
